Privacy Policy — Bus Driver Journal

Last updated: November 2026

1. No Central Server / Your Data Stays Yours

Bus Driver Journal is a free, independent personal logging utility for bus drivers. It is not affiliated with any transport operator. We do not operate a backend server and never collect, view, or store your driving logs, bus numbers, photos, or notes.

2. Google Drive Access (drive.file)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests only the limited https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This allows the app to read and write solely the folder and backup file it creates inside your own Google Drive (Bus_Driver_Journal_Data/journals_db.json). It cannot access any other files in your Google Drive.

3. Local Storage & Data Retention

Records are cached locally on your device for offline access. You may configure automatic or prompted cleanup (default: 1 year) and delete all data at any time.
